Gerard Way

Role: Lead vocals, primary lyricist   Years: 2001–2013, 2019–present

Gerard studied art at the School of Visual Arts in New York before forming My Chemical Romance in the aftermath of the September 11 attacks, channeling what he witnessed into the band's first song, "Skylines and Turnstiles."

Beyond the band: Released the solo album Hesitant Alien in 2014, and writes the comic series The Umbrella Academy, later adapted into a Netflix show.

Ray Toro

Role: Lead guitar, backing vocals   Years: 2001–2013, 2019–present

Ray played in a local New Jersey band with future MCR drummer Matt Pelissier before the two of them, Gerard, and eventually Mikey formed the band. He's often compared to Queen's Brian May, a major influence on his playing, and actually performed alongside May at the 2011 Reading and Leeds Festivals.

Beyond the band: Released a solo album, Remember the Laughter, in 2016.

Frank Iero

Role: Rhythm guitar, backing vocals   Years: 2002–2013, 2019–present

Before joining MCR, Frank fronted the punk band Pencey Prep — and was actually a fan of the band's earliest demos, helping them land some of their first shows before ever joining himself.

Beyond the band: The most prolific solo artist of the group, with projects spanning Leathermouth, Death Spells, LS Dunes, and a run of albums as frnkiero andthe cellabration, Frank Iero and the Patience, and Frank Iero and the Future Violents — see the Side Projects page.

Mikey Way

Role: Bass   Years: 2001–2013, 2019–present

Gerard's younger brother actually named the band — he spotted the words "chemical romance" in a novel while working at a bookstore and added "My" to it. He'd originally auditioned for Frank Iero's band, Pencey Prep, before joining his brother's new project instead.

Beyond the band: Formed the band Electric Century with musician David Debiak after the 2013 breakup, releasing an EP and a full album.

Bob Bryar

Role: drummer   Years: 2004-2010

Bob replaced original drummer Matt Pelissier in mid-2004, just as the band was breaking through, and played on both Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge (2004) and The Black Parade (2006) — his powerful, precise drumming is a huge part of why those records hit so hard. He left the band in March 2010, before Danger Days was recorded.

Matt Pelissier

Role: original drummer   Years: 2001-2004

Matt co-founded the band with Gerard Way — the two rehearsed in his parents' attic in the earliest days, and he played every show of the band's underground era. He's the drummer on I Brought You My Bullets, You Brought Me Your Love (2002), and he brought his friend Ray Toro into the band. He departed in 2004, shortly after Three Cheers was released, and was replaced by Bob Bryar.
