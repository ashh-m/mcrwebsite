Pencey Prep

Years: 1998-2002   Genre: Indie-punk / early emo

Key release: Heartbreak in Stereo (2001, Eyeball Records)   Start with: 10 Rings, Trying to Escape the Inevitable, Flordia Plates

Before MCR existed, Frank fronted this New Jersey underground band as lead singer and primary songwriter. They signed to Eyeball Records, the same label that launched MCR before breaking up in 2002. Frank was a fan of MCR's earliest demos and helped them get shows before joining himself.

Leathermouth

Years: 2008-2010   Genre: Hardcore Punk

Key release: XO (2009, Epitah Records)   Start with: TODO

While MCR was reaching global stardom, Frank needed a raw outlet for his roots in heavy hardcore. As Leathermouth's lead singer and lyricist he pushed a harsher vocal style and darker, politically charged themes than anything MCR was doing at the time. The band released one album and went quiet by 2010
