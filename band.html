My Chemical Romance: The Full Story

My Chemical Romance formed in Newark, New Jersey, in 2001, after vocalist Gerard Way witnessed the September 11 attacks and decided to make music instead of pursuing comics. What started as a rough, emotional debut on an indie label became one of the defining rock bands of the 2000s

Through the Years

Click the arrows or dots below to move through the band's history.

2001–2002: Formation & Underground

2001
September

Gerard Way forms the band with Matt Pelissier after witnessing the September 11th attacks. Ray Toro and Mikey Way join soon after.

2002
July

Frank Iero joins on rhythm guitar just before the independent album I Brought You My Bullets, You Brought Me Your Love is released on Eyeball Records.

2004–2006: Breakthrough

2004
June

Major label debut Three Cheers For Sweet Revenge is released, breaking the band into the mainstream.

2004
July

Bob Bryar replaces Matt Pelissier on drums.

2006
October

The concept album The Black Parade is released and becomes the band's defining work.

2010–2013: Danger Days & Departure

2010
March

Bob Bryar leaves the band.

2010
November

The band's fourth studio album Danger Days: The True Lives of the Fabulous Killjoys is released.

2013
March

The band announces its breakup.

2019–2022: Reunion

2019
October

The band announces a reunion tour.

2019
December

The band plays their comeback show in Los Angeles.

2020–2021

The reunion tour is postponed several times due to the Covid-19 pandemic.

2022
May

"The Foundations of Decay" is randomly released after 8 years of no new music.

2024–2025: Present Day

2024
October

The band headlines When We Were Young Festival, performing The Black Parade in its entirety for the first time in 17 years.

2025
June

A remixed and remastered deluxe edition of Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge is released, 21 years after the original album.

2025
July

The "Long Live the Black Parade" stadium tour kicks off.
